Handwritten Digit Recognizer

Draw a single digit from 0 to 9 and a small neural network (a CNN) guesses it as you draw. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you draw is sent anywhere.

Tip: draw one digit, big and centred, with one clear stroke width. The model learned from digits written with a pen on paper, so neat, simple shapes work best.

What the model sees. Your drawing is cropped, shrunk to fit inside a 20 by 20 box, and centred in a 28 by 28 grid, the same format as the MNIST training images.

A CNN trained on the MNIST dataset (60,000 handwritten digits), about 99.1% accurate on its test set. Built by Martins (MartCruz17) for the CodeAlpha Machine Learning internship.
